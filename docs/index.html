01 / Course promise

For instructional designers, faculty partners, and co-curricular leaders

Leave with a bounded claim, a tested learning path, and evidence for a responsible pilot.

This independent professional-learning resource helps designers move from a promising idea to a proposal reviewers can inspect. Every lesson produces a reusable component of the final dossier rather than a disposable exercise.

  • Evidence-led opportunity and learner-journey research
  • Traceable outcomes, practice, assessment, and badge evidence
  • Prototype, accessibility, pilot, and governance planning
  • A bounded recommendation for review and implementation planning

05 / Deliverables

The finished work

A concise Proposal Dossier—not a decorative course mockup.

The final submission organizes nine decision-ready sections and connects each claim to learner need, evidence, experience design, evaluation, and operational ownership.

Build the dossier in the live Fieldbook
  1. Executive recommendation and requested decision
  2. Opportunity brief and learner evidence
  3. Competency claim, boundaries, and prerequisites
  4. Alignment and module sequence
  5. Assessment blueprint and credential metadata
  6. Interaction-pattern rationale and prototype evidence
  7. Pilot protocol, measures, and decision thresholds
  8. Accessibility and mobile readiness audit
  9. Governance, lifecycle, risks, and open authority questions
